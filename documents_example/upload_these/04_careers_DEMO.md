# CodertechPRO Careers - Open Positions

CodertechPRO ALGO & Robotics lists two open positions on its website. Applications are made through the enquiry form on codertechpro.com by choosing "Career Application" in the service list.

## Algo Trading Tutor / Instructor
- Type: full-time or part-time.
- Location: Kalamassery, Kochi, or hybrid.
- Role: educate and mentor aspiring algorithmic traders, and teach Python-based strategy design, backtesting frameworks and broker API integration.
- Requirements: proficiency in Python and technical market analysis; experience with trading APIs such as Zerodha, Angel One and MT5; strong communication skills in English and Malayalam.

## Algo Trading Developer
- Type: full-time.
- Location: Kalamassery, Kochi, or remote.
- Role: build custom trading bots, MQL5 bridges, WebSocket integrations and automated execution engines for client strategies.
- Requirements: solid background in Python, Node.js and/or MQL5; hands-on experience with WebSockets and REST APIs; understanding of financial order types and execution speed.
