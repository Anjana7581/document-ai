# CodertechPRO Payment, Eligibility, Certificates and Policies

This document explains payment, eligibility, certificates, internship, placement, refunds, the risk disclaimer and the privacy policy of CodertechPRO ALGO & Robotics in Kalamassery, Kochi. Course fees are listed in the courses document. For anything not covered here, call or WhatsApp +91 9037010647.

## Payment and installments
CodertechPRO accepts online payment by UPI, debit or credit card, and net banking. For the Python Algo Class (Master Course), the Rs. 20,000 fee can be paid in 2 installments: Rs. 10,000 at joining and Rs. 10,000 within the first month. For the Master Program, the fee can be paid in 4 installments of Rs. 25,000. Students who join in a group of 3 or more get a 10% discount. The fees include certificates, recorded sessions and the software used in class.

## Eligibility and requirements
Anyone who has completed 10th standard or above can join the CodertechPRO courses. A student who has not passed 10th standard should call or WhatsApp +91 9037010647 to discuss. For the Python Algo Class, a student needs a laptop and basic knowledge of IT and computers. The laptop should have at least 8 GB of RAM, Windows or Mac, and an internet connection. The software used (Python and VS Code) is free. No trading experience or trading account is needed to join.

## Certificates, internship and placement
Students of the Python Algo Class receive a course completion certificate and an internship certificate. The internship is a 4-week project-based internship, online or offline, and unpaid. Placement support includes resume help, interview preparation, and referrals to partner companies. CodertechPRO does not guarantee a job.

## Recorded sessions and doubt clearance
Recorded sessions are available for every class, and there is a morning doubt clearance session where students can get their questions answered.

## Refund and cancellation policy
A student can request a refund of the CodertechPRO course fee within 3 days of joining, provided no more than one class has been attended. To request a refund, call or WhatsApp +91 9037010647 or visit the Kalamassery lab. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. After the 3-day period, the fee is not refundable, but the student may ask to move to a later batch at no extra charge.

Students and clients are responsible for their own trading decisions and for the capital they use.

## Privacy policy
When someone fills in an enquiry or callback form on the CodertechPRO website, CodertechPRO collects their name, phone number or email address, and message details. This information is used only to reply to the enquiry about courses or custom development. It is not sold to anyone. To ask CodertechPRO to remove your personal data, call or WhatsApp +91 9037010647.
