# CodertechPRO Courses, Fees and Schedule

CodertechPRO ALGO & Robotics (CoderTechPro Labs, Quantitative Trading Lab) in Kalamassery, Kochi offers three courses. Admissions are open for 2026. The courses follow a hybrid model: students can learn offline at the lab at Pallath Tower, Kochi, or join the live classes online. CodertechPRO does not sell tips or signals; it teaches students to build their own trading systems. All classes are taught in English and Malayalam, and each batch has about 20 students. A free demo class is available; call or WhatsApp +91 9037010647 to book it.

## The three CodertechPRO courses and their fees

1. Python + AI + Algorithmic Trading Master Course (also called the Python Algo Class): beginner level, 12 weeks (3 months), live lab, fee Rs. 20,000.
2. Python + AI + Algorithmic Trading Master Program (Beginner to Advanced): advanced level, 24 weeks, live lab, fee Rs. 1,00,000 (one lakh rupees).
3. Complete Beginner Robotics Course Roadmap: beginner level, 8 weeks, live lab, fee Rs. 30,000.

## Course 1: Python + AI + Algorithmic Trading Master Course (Python Algo Class)

This CodertechPRO course teaches Python, Artificial Intelligence and algorithmic trading through hands-on, project-based learning, for beginners, developers, traders and aspiring AI engineers.

- Duration: 12 weeks (3 months), live lab. Fee: Rs. 20,000.
- Schedule: Monday to Friday, about 1.5 hours per class, live batch at 7 PM. The next batch starts on December 1st.
- Recorded sessions are available, and there is a morning doubt clearance session.
- Certificates: course completion certificate and internship certificate. Placement support is available.
- Requirements: a laptop and basic knowledge of IT and computers.

### Master Course syllabus (12 weeks)
- Weeks 1 to 2: trading basics, stock market charts, and Python fundamentals.
- Weeks 3 to 4: Pandas, handling market data, and building charts.
- Weeks 5 to 6: indicators, building strategies, and backtesting on past data.
- Weeks 7 to 8: connecting to brokers such as Zerodha, Angel One and MT5, automatic order placement with stop-loss, and live price streaming.
- Weeks 9 to 10: risk management (maximum loss limits, preventing duplicate trades) and using AI tools for data analysis and strategy ideas.
- Weeks 11 to 12: running bots on cloud servers (AWS or Linux), WhatsApp and Telegram trade alerts, and the final project.

### What the Python Algo Class teaches (6 steps)
Step 1 - Trading Basics and Concepts: what algo trading is, how charts and live prices work, and turning chart patterns into automatic buy and sell rules.
Step 2 - Easy Python Coding: beginner-friendly Python, backtesting, and buy and sell signal triggers.
Step 3 - Broker Account Connection: connecting code to Zerodha, Angel One and MT5, automatic order placement with an exact stop-loss, and streaming live prices.
Step 4 - Risk Control and Safety: maximum loss limits, preventing duplicate or accidental trades, and checking signal quality.
Step 5 - Speed and Reliability: faster bots without lag, smooth and safe code, and handling multiple stocks or pairs.
Step 6 - 24/7 Cloud Automated Trading: running bots on AWS or Linux servers, and instant WhatsApp and Telegram alerts.
Tools covered: Python, Pandas, WebSockets, REST APIs, broker APIs, QuantConnect and backtesting.
