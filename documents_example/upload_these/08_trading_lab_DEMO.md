# CodertechPRO Trading Lab

The Trading Lab on the CodertechPRO website (codertechpro.com/lab) is described as one platform for every trader, with three environments.

## Paper Trading (live)
The Paper Trading environment is live. It offers simulated NSE and NFO execution with real profit and loss, margin, take-profit and stop-loss, and portfolio tracking. No real capital is at risk. A login is required to open the dashboard.

## Algo Trading (coming soon)
The website lists Algo Trading as coming soon. It is described as a place to connect brokers, deploy Python strategies, backtest, and automate execution with risk controls.

## PineScript Lab (coming soon)
The website lists the PineScript Lab as coming soon, with beta access. It is described as a place to write TradingView PineScript, convert it to Python execution logic, and test it on the paper trading engine.

## Availability and login
The Paper Trading platform is free for enrolled CodertechPRO students, and the login is created after enrollment. The Algo Trading lab and the PineScript Lab are coming soon, and launch dates will be announced. For help with the login, call or WhatsApp CodertechPRO on +91 9037010647.
