# CodertechPRO ALGO & Robotics - Company Profile

CodertechPRO ALGO & Robotics (the name is also written as CoderTechPro or CodertechPRO) is an algo trading training and software company based in Kalamassery, Kochi, Kerala, India. Its website describes it as a quantitative trading lab and development agency, and brands its training as CoderTechPro Labs, Quantitative Trading Lab. The name in the website footer is CodertechPRO ALGO & Robotics LLP.

## What CodertechPRO does

CodertechPRO offers three main things:
1. Courses: three live-lab courses in Python, AI, algorithmic trading and robotics (see the courses document), taught offline at the Kalamassery center in Kochi and also online.
2. Strategy development, which means turning a trader's rules into automatic Python strategies and testing them.
3. Custom trading bot development and trading signal automation for Indian brokers and global platforms.

## How CodertechPRO teaches

CodertechPRO says it does not sell trading tips or signals. It teaches students how to build, backtest and deploy their own trading systems, so that they can create their own edge. Its training follows a hybrid model: students can learn at the Pallath Tower lab in Kochi or join live online. Admissions are open for 2026.

## Brokers and platforms CodertechPRO works with

CodertechPRO works with Zerodha, Angel One, Interactive Brokers and MetaTrader 5 (MT5). It also works with TradingView alerts and QuantConnect strategies.

## What CodertechPRO promises to students and clients

- Practical code exercises: in the Python algo class, students write real strategy code that connects to Indian and international brokers.
- Automated strategy rules: clear programmatic entry rules, exit conditions and automatic risk limits, to remove trading emotions.
- In-person support in Kochi: direct help and code debugging at the Kalamassery center.
